# SERIES BIBLE: "The Hollow Hours"

**Format:** short first-person horror stories told by an ordinary person about
something wrong that happens during the quiet hours of the night (11 PM - 5 AM).
Stories come in arcs of several parts. Each part is ~75-95 seconds when narrated.

**Voice & tone**
- Plain, spoken English. Short sentences. Concrete details (times, objects, sounds).
- Understated dread. The narrator is rational, tired, and slowly realizes the rules are off.
- Never explain the monster. Imply. Let the listener's imagination do the work.
- No jump-scare language ("suddenly!"), no gore, no cursing.

**Structure of every episode**
1. Hook (first sentence): a strange, specific claim that raises a question.
2. Build: 2-3 concrete beats that escalate.
3. Turn: one detail that makes the listener's skin crawl.
4. Ending: cliffhanger (parts 1..N-1) or resolution with one last chill (part N).

**Continuity rules**
- Each arc has its own narrator, place and mystery. Arcs do not need to connect.
- Inside an arc, keep names, places and rules consistent with previous parts.
- A recurring easter egg across ALL arcs: something is always 3:07 AM.

**Hard no's**
- No real people, brands, existing franchises, or real tragedies.
- No sexual content, no harm to children, no self-harm, no hate.
- Do not copy or closely imitate known creepypastas.
