# CHANNEL BIBLE: "MARIGOLD LETTERS"

**What it is:** short, emotionally specific text slideshows (about 65 seconds) over soft photos and warm
music. Gentle, honest reminders about love, self-worth, character and growth.
People save them, send them to someone, and comment because a line felt like it was written for them.

**What makes a video good (this is what separates it from the thousands of generic quote videos)**
- SPECIFIC beats generic. "You stopped checking if he'd text back" beats "Love yourself".
- A real emotional ARC: wound or doubt -> turning point -> calm, earned hope. Never preachy.
- Second person ("you") or first person ("I"). Short sentences. Everyday, concrete images.
- Warm, never cheesy. No clichés like "love is blind", "follow your heart", "live laugh love".
- ONE line per video should be quotable on its own (put it near the end).

**Voice**
- Plain, tender, a little vulnerable. Like a friend talking quietly at night.
- No hustle-culture, no gender wars, no "alpha/queen" language, no manipulation tactics.
- Professional/ethical themes keep the same warm tone: integrity, kindness at work, patience, purpose.

**Hard no's**
- NEVER quote, paraphrase or imitate famous quotes, songs, books or real people. 100% original wording.
- No attributions ("- Maya Angelou"), no real names, no brands.
- No medical/psychological claims, no advice to cut off people, no revenge, no self-harm themes.
- No sexual content.
